Onboarding: I meet everyone before we start

I meet one on one with every person on your sprint team, so I hear what they know and what worries them before we're in a room together. You pick your Decider, the one person who owns the outcome, and everyone gets set up on the Miro board on their own time.

You leave with: A team that's heard, ready and working from the first minute.

Alignment: Get everyone on the same problem

Your experts talk through the challenge. We map the full customer experience first, then turn every pain point into a question worth solving and prioritize what matters most. Together we set one ambitious goal and name the biggest questions standing in the way. Your Decider circles the one spot we'll focus on.

You leave with: One goal, the questions that matter most and a clear target.

Solution: Find inspiration. Unlock creativity.

We find and share inspiration on how others solve the same kind of problem. Then I guide everyone through time-boxed creative exercises, grounded in creative psychology, so each person unlocks their own genius and builds a clear solution of their own. No drawing skills needed.

You leave with: A wall of solid ideas, one from every person in the room.

Decision: Vote, decide, move on

The team marks the best parts of every concept, then votes in silence so the loudest voice doesn't win. Your Decider makes the final call on one direction, and together we storyboard exactly what a customer will see and do.

You leave with: One direction and a storyboard with no open questions.

Prototype: We build what you chose

Our team turns the storyboard into a realistic prototype your customers can click through. You're free to get back to your week while we check in with your Decider along the way and line up people for feedback.

You leave with: A prototype that looks and feels real, ready for feedback.

User testing: Customers put it to the test

We test the prototype and the idea with your customers and people in your target market, so you hear what works, what doesn't and whether they want it. Your team can watch or review the feedback.

You leave with: Knowing whether your customers want this solution before you invest to build it.

Handover: What comes next

We walk your team through the findings and recommend whether to build, rework or stop. You leave with the work we made and a clear plan.

You leave with: The findings, the prototype and a clear next move.
